## Software Feedback Boundary

The matrix maps the current experimental evidence into CityLBM decisions:

- Experiment 1 / AIJ Case A remains a default release-quality guard for the workflow.
- Experiment 2 / AIJ Case E keeps the official z=2 m, 80 ac+N probes, and `raw_trilinear` as the formal protocol.
- `Diagnostic LBM Nu Override`, `Diagnostic Z Origin Offset`, and non-raw probe sampling modes remain diagnostic switches.
- The official Case E accuracy upgrade remains blocked by the metric gate and runtime/load-identity readiness gates.
- Experiment 3 / TUM2TWIN remains a digital-twin screening/application layer, not Case E benchmark accuracy evidence.

## Not Claimed

This release candidate does not claim:

- AIJ Case E predictive accuracy;
- mesh independence;
- LES improvement;
- field validation for Experiment 3;
- annual comfort or safety compliance;
- Rhino/Grasshopper loading of the newly built GHA;
- formal `v0.4.0` readiness.
