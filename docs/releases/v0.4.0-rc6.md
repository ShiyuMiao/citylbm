## Interpretation

Alternative near-wall probe sampling reduces underprediction bias and improves
the diagnostic correlation, but every R2 remains negative. This confirms that
near-wall/probe-protocol treatment is part of the error mechanism, while also
showing that sampling alone is insufficient for paper-grade predictive accuracy.
