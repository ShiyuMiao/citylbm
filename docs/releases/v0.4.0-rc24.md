## Paper Result Boundary

The packet consolidates:

- Experiment 1 / AIJ Case A workflow smoke-regression evidence;
- Experiment 2 / AIJ Case E official z=2 m negative-validation evidence;
- Experiment 2 software-feedback evidence from default-policy and failure-mode gates;
- Experiment 3 / TUM2TWIN digital-twin screening and application evidence;
- blocked claims that must remain in limitations.

It separates manuscript-ready rows from limitations-only and blocked rows. It does not add a new solver run, change official z=2 m metrics, or permit the formal `v0.4.0` tag.

## Not Claimed

This release candidate does not claim:

- AIJ Case E predictive accuracy;
- mesh independence;
- LES improvement;
- field validation for Experiment 3;
- annual comfort or safety compliance;
- Rhino/Grasshopper loading of the newly built GHA;
- formal `v0.4.0` readiness.
